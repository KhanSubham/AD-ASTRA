THE LIFE CYCLE OF A STAR

How celestial bodies are born, live, and die.

1

STELLAR NURSERY (NEBULA)

Stars begin their lives in massive clouds of dust and gas called nebulae. Gravity slowly pulls this material together, creating dense, hot cores.

MAIN SEQUENCE

Once nuclear fusion ignites in the core, the star stabilizes. It spends the majority of its life fusing hydrogen into helium, just like our Sun is doing right now.

2
3

RED GIANT PHASE

As the star exhausts its hydrogen, the core contracts while the outer layers expand and cool, turning the star into a massive, glowing red giant.

STELLAR REMNANT

Depending on its mass, the star will end its life violently as a supernova, leaving behind a dense White Dwarf, a spinning Neutron Star, or a gravitational Black Hole.
